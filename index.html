01 — A little about me

Curious by default.

I'm an AI researcher and engineer working on retrieval-augmented generation and document understanding for large language models.

For more than eight years I've worked on applied AI. I started as an AI scientist at Course5i, then moved into machine learning engineering at yellow.ai, where I now lead the engineering of the Document Cognition product.

My research looks at how documents should be chunked, ingested and retrieved so language models give grounded, accurate answers. I'm based in the United Arab Emirates and always happy to talk about RAG, document AI and LLMs.

GraphRAGMulti-hop QAEfficiency

Lightfoot: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Exact, Model-Free Document Deletion

U Allu

A GraphRAG system where deleting a document is an exact database operation with no model calls. Across MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA and HotpotQA it builds the cheapest index of the systems compared: 26–31% cheaper than HippoRAG 2 and 3–6× cheaper than LightRAG and fast-graphrag.
